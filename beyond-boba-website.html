Two shops, one standard

Beyond Boba started with a simple idea: Taiwanese tea and premium flavours, in perfect balance. No pre-mixed powders pretending to be tea, no pearls sitting around going stiff.

You'll find us on Ilford Lane and in Whitechapel — same recipes, same standard, both shops.

  • Pearls cooked fresh, not pre-made and reheated
  • Brown sugar cooked in-house, not syrup from a bottle
  • Tea brewed daily, never sitting for days
